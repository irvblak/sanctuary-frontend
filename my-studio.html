What goes where?

First choose the hat you are wearing. Then tell the Studio what kind of work you want to create.

1

Dated & Happening

Something taking place on a particular date goes to the Calendar, Events List and Notice. It does not go into the Library.

2

Useful & Lasting

You create a piece of written work which, depending on the hat you are wearing when you enter the Studio, automatically goes to the appropriate Section in the Library.

If it is intended to teach or explain something, it goes to two places under your name:

Our School, where lessons, guides and manuals from all Role Holders are available in one Section; and the Section connected with the hat you are wearing, where work from Role Holders past and present is available for everyone to read.

3

Interesting & Creative

Here you have the choice of creating something purely for your own pleasure or something intended for the Library.

If you are wearing your Writer or Artist hat, you send your work to an Editor. The Editor will either place it in the Library Reading Room or Gallery, or get back to you for discussion.

All other Studio work goes directly to the appropriate Library Section.

That is all you need to decide.
The website knows where to put the work.
