Sanctuary Club Role Register

The Role Register shows the members currently helping to run the Club, its activities, publications, website and Residents Association.

Some roles may be shared by more than one member. Activity Organisers are shown against the particular activities they organise, and Website Helpers & Tutors against their specialisms.

This is a live Register. Changes made by an Administrator to current role assignments appear here automatically.
