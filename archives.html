Sanctuary Club

Our Archives

A growing record of Sanctuary life — bringing together useful documents, important decisions, community news and memories that residents may wish to revisit.

The archive will develop gradually as earlier material is gathered and new information is added. Everything will be organised clearly, so that residents can find what they need without having to search through long lists of files.

Committee and Panel records

The Club Committee and the Residents Association Panel serve different purposes. Each will therefore have its own equal and clearly identified part of the archive.

🏡

Club Committee Archive

Club life and activities

This section will preserve information connected with the running of Sanctuary Club and the activities it supports.

  • Club Committee information
  • Past Club announcements
  • Event and activity records
  • News sheets and community updates
  • Useful Club documents
What is planned?
Material will be added progressively as documents are prepared, approved or selected for long-term reference.

Residents Association Panel Archive

Association and resident matters

This section will provide residents with an organised record of Residents Association business and matters affecting the wider Sanctuary community.

  • Community Meeting and AGM documents
  • Meeting agendas and approved records
  • Consultations and resident information
  • Election material
  • Communications with Sanctuary Management
What is planned?

More than documents

In time, the archive can also help preserve the story of Sanctuary: photographs, past events, community projects, acknowledgements and memories contributed by residents. It should become both a useful reference and a friendly record of our shared community life.

Watch this space...

The links are being put in place now so that each archive can grow in an orderly way. New sections will open as useful material is gathered and ready to share.

🔒 Privacy and care

Some Association records may contain information intended only for Sanctuary residents. Appropriate protection will be applied before any private material is made available here.

Sanctuary Club is designed to support everyone who helps our community.
