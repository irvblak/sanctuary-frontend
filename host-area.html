Creative Canvas — quick guide

There is no wrong order. Add something, click it, then change or move it.

Title or TextClick inside the words to type or edit. The Text tools appear automatically.
MoveSelect a box, then drag its Move tab. Blue guide lines briefly appear when centred.
ResizeDrag any dot on a selected box — corners resize both ways; side dots resize one way.
IllustrationsAdd up to two per A4 page. Use Fit whole image or Fill box.
Draw / PaintAdd a drawing, choose Pencil, Line, Rectangle, Oval, Fill or Eraser, then choose line colour and thickness.
PagesEvery page stays A4. Add, duplicate, reorder and optionally number pages automatically.

Tip: click a blank part of the page when you have finished with a box. The selection handles disappear, but your work stays exactly where you put it.

Hands-on is the quickest way to learn: click a box to select it; click inside Title/Text to type; use Move to position it; drag any edge or corner dot to resize. Drawings have their own Pencil/Paint tools. Page size always remains A4.
