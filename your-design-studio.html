What happens to my Studio work?

It depends on the hat you are wearing when you create something — and what you want to happen when you are satisfied with it.

I want to just be creative

If it is a piece of writing or art you have created purely for your own pleasure, you can keep it in your personal Archive here on the website, or transfer it to your computer and read it or gaze admiringly at it in splendid isolation.

OR — and why not do this instead?

You can submit it to the Editor for approval. It might then be placed in the Library Reading Room or Gallery for everyone to enjoy.

Why not teach or join a Computer Art class? Who knows, you might even have your very own Exhibition one day. 😉

I want to be informative

If you are wearing an Event Host, Activity Organiser, Teacher, Club Committee or Residents Association hat, the Studio knows what you are working on and where it may need to go.

You can announce an event, activity or class through the Calendar and Events List, and use the Studio to create the accompanying poster or notice — then really go to town with all the Studio tools.
