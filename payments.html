Sanctuary Club

Financial

This area will explore how Sanctuary Club can help members, Hosts and the Treasurer manage Club finances simply, securely and privately.

What is planned?

As Sanctuary Club develops, we hope this area will gradually bring together services such as:

  • Event payments
  • Payment confirmations and receipts
  • Personal transaction history
  • Refund information
  • Financial information for Hosts
  • Treasurer facilities
  • Secure payment options, where appropriate

Exploring the best way forward

There is still important investigative work to do, including deciding how Club funds should be held and which external financial arrangements would be suitable.

Any facilities introduced here will need to be practical, properly authorised and appropriate for Sanctuary Club.

Watch this space... Plans will be introduced gradually as suitable arrangements are investigated and agreed.

🔒 Financial privacy

Personal financial information will be treated as private. Any future services will be designed so that members see only the information appropriate to them, while authorised Hosts and the Treasurer have only the access needed for their responsibilities.

Sanctuary Club is designed to support everyone who helps our community.
