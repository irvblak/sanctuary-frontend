Your Information

For your privacy only the fields you fill in will be shown in the Members Directory.

All residents where you live have the same membership number (e.g. SM55, SC03).

You can add up to four residents for each property. Each member/resident will have a separate ID (e.g. SMxx/2) and this will serve to identify residents who become Administrators, Committee members, Hosts etc.

PIN and Members Directory access
All memberships begin with the community default PIN. Use your membership number and 4-digit PIN to see or change your contact information, or open the Members Directory to contact a neighbour. You will be able to choose your own PIN once PIN change is enabled.
