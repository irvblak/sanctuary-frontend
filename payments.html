Financial

Financial will gradually bring together the services that help members, Hosts and the Treasurer manage Club finances simply, securely and privately.

As Sanctuary Club develops, this area is expected to include:

  • Event payments
  • Payment confirmations and receipts
  • Your personal transaction history
  • Refund information
  • Host financial summaries
  • Treasurer facilities
  • Future online payment services

The links and foundations are already being prepared. New facilities will appear here as they become ready.
