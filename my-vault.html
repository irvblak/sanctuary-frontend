What happens to your work

Work in Progress is kept here in your Vault and can be reopened and continued whenever you want to carry on.

Once you are happy with it and would like it to be published, the computer automatically places it in the appropriate Library section or sends it to the Curator or Editor:

  • If it’s Art, it goes to the Curator, who will approve it and place it in the Gallery or get back to you to discuss it.
  • Similarly, if it’s written work, your Editor will either send it to the Reading Room or get back to you.
  • If you are an Activity Organiser or Teacher and want to let members know when an event or class is happening, it goes immediately on the Calendar and into the Events List.
  • If you want to update a Guide or publish a Notice, it will go directly to the appropriate Library section.
