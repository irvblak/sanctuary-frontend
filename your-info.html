Sanctuary Club and the community

Sanctuary already has residents who organise coffee mornings, bridge, quizzes, lunches, book clubs, walks and many other activities. Sanctuary Club has been created to support that community—not to change it.

Existing organisers remain in charge Activities can continue exactly as they do now. The website is simply another helping hand whenever it is useful.
Old and new can run together Paper notices, word of mouth, telephone calls and familiar arrangements can continue alongside the website indefinitely.
New ideas are always welcome Sanctuary Club can help a resident share an idea, find others who are interested and get something new started.
Taking part matters too Organising is only one way to contribute. Coming along, welcoming neighbours and enjoying what others arrange are every bit as important.

Residents who already organise something are warmly invited to use whatever parts of Sanctuary Club make life easier. There is no pressure to change anything that works well and no need to surrender the identity or independence of an existing activity.

Anyone with a new idea—or simply a thought worth discussing—can speak to an Administrator member, who will be pleased to help with the practicalities.

The website does not create the community. Residents do.
Sanctuary Club simply helps neighbours find one another, share information and enjoy more of what makes Sanctuary a friendly place to live.
