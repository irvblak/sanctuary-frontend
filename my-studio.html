What happens to my Studio work?

It depends on the hat you are wearing when you create something and what you want to happen when you are satisfied with it.

I want to just be creative

If it is a piece of writing or art you have created purely for your own pleasure, you can keep the editable work safely in My Vault and return to it whenever you wish.

OR — and why not do this instead?

You can submit it to the Editor for approval. It might then be placed in the Library Reading Room or Gallery for everyone to enjoy.

Why not teach or join a Computer Art class? Who knows, you might even have your very own Exhibition one day. 😉

I want to be informative

If you are wearing your Event Host, Activity Organiser, Teacher, Club Committee, Residents Association or Condo Admin hat, the Studio knows the capacity in which you are working and can guide your work towards the right destination.

Dated events, activities and classes can go to the Calendar & Events List with their related poster or Notice. Permanent information can be sent to its proper Library shelf.

You concentrate on the content and the design. The computer does the filing.
