🔒 Your Privacy

The directory and other private services are intended only for Sanctuary residents. That is why we ask you to replace the temporary 2468 Starter PIN to your personal Private PIN before accessing these areas. The 2468 Starter PIN is used only to help new residents join Sanctuary Club and to help existing residents if their PIN ever needs to be reset. Once you have chosen your own Private PIN, only that PIN will provide access to your private member services. Only the minimum personal information needed to operate Sanctuary Club is held, and it is used solelyfor the benefit of our residents and community.

🛡 Security & Data Protection

  • Administrators cannot view your personal Membership PIN.
  • If a PIN is forgotten, it can be reset but never recovered.
  • An automatic forgotten-PIN process is planned for a later stage.
  • Residents Association Panel information will have its own separately protected area where appropriate.

🤝 Getting Started

If you are unsure where to begin, start with Your Information. Resident helpers will be able to assist anyone who would like a little support getting comfortable with the site.
