Which cap will you don?

When you enter your studio, decide which cap you want to don, then decide where you want your work to go.

Calendar

If it’s to tell members when your event is happening, send it to the Calendar.

Library Education

If it’s to explain how to do something connected with your activity or role, it’ll go on the appropriate shelf in the Library Education section.

Library Reading Room

If it’s to do with your activity or role and just for general information, it will go to the appropriate shelf in the Library Reading Room.

Your Writer’s cap

If you write a story, anecdote or article wearing your Writer’s cap, it will go to your Editor, who will publish it under your name and send it on to the Reading Room, or get back to you for a chat about it. Letters to the Panel go to the Mews or Court Secretary, according to your membership.

Your Artist’s cap

If you create a piece of artwork wearing your Artist’s cap, you can keep it just for your own enjoyment or submit it to the Curator for consideration for inclusion in the Gallery.

Teaching work by Writers and Artists follows the same Editor or Curator route to Library Education.

Whatever you decide to be or do, it’s all there to be enjoyed.
